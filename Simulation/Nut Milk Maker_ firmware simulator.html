Nut Milk Maker: firmware simulator

A behavioural model of main.c (all nine orders and the workaround modes) with the Arduino Nano, LCD1602, buttons, lid switch, NTC, heater relay, motor driver and buzzer. Press and hold buttons with the mouse or finger. Not a cycle-accurate AVR emulator.

Arduino pin signals (logic analyser view)

Buttons idle HIGH (pull-up) and go LOW when pressed. D4 LOW means lid closed. D8 and D10 are on/off. D9 is the PWM duty in %. A0 is the NTC divider voltage (0 V when the sensor is open).

D9 at microsecond scale: 20 kHz PWM (50 µs period, TOP = 799), three periods shown.
